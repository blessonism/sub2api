# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Sub2API users and administrators managing API access, usage, billing, and promotional activities. Users need to understand their current eligibility and verify that lottery participation is transparent and fair.

## Product Purpose

Provide a dependable API management platform with clear operational data and auditable promotional activities. Success means users can understand their status and complete common actions without ambiguity.

## Brand Personality

Clear, dependable, and energetic when an activity calls for it. Operational screens stay restrained; campaign moments may use stronger color and motion when they communicate real state.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative casino styling, opaque probability claims, dense email-chip walls, and interfaces where animation obscures the actual draw state. Avoid marketing composition inside authenticated workflows.

## Design Principles

- Show the source of truth: probabilities, weights, eligibility, and timing must be inspectable.
- Highlight personal relevance without hiding the shared public state.
- Use motion to communicate draw state, never as permanent decoration.
- Keep operational actions familiar and predictable.
- Preserve privacy through consistent identity masking.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast. Never encode weight or personal identity through color alone. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep all eligibility and probability information available as text.
